
# Drafting a Thesis Statement

**Craft a Purpose Statement**
- Declaration of your specific idea
- should identify topic and goal
- should be specific and concise

**Craft a Thesis Statement**:
- A thesis statement is a one sentence version of the message in speech // goal of speech
	- Be concrete
	- Make a statement not question
	- Treat thesis as a WIP
	- Tell The Truth

**Examples:**
- Although sales of herbal supplements are growing, medical research shows most of their effects to be nothing more than placebos
- Because gold prices rise even in a weak economy, investing in gold is a sound financial decision.

# Transitions

Help your speech follow smoothly; provide a path for listeners to follow

**Types of Transitions:**
- Previews
	- next let's discuss..
- Summaries
	- So far, we have learned....
- Signposts
	- For example....
- Nonverbal
	- Movement, gestures, or pauses.
